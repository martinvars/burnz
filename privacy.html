Burnz

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Burnz collects no data. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you do in Burnz leaves your iPhone.

Apple Health

With your permission, Burnz reads Active Energy from Apple Health on your iPhone to turn the calories you burn into minutes of AI. Burnz never writes to Apple Health and reads no other health data. Health data is never used for advertising and is never shared.

Screen Time

With your permission, Burnz uses Apple's Screen Time framework to hide the AI apps you choose and block their websites until you spend earned minutes. Burnz cannot see your app usage, messages or browsing.

Data on your device

Your exchange rate, unlock history and settings are stored only on your iPhone and are deleted when you delete Burnz.

Children

Burnz does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: contact us.

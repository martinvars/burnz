Burnz

Want AI? Move.

Burnz turns the calories you burn — running, cycling, padel, walking, any sport — into minutes of AI. Your AI apps (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and more) stay hidden on your iPhone until you spend the minutes you've earned.

Support

The AI apps disappeared. That's Burnz working. Open Burnz and tap 15 min, 30 min or 1 hour on the Move tab to show them for that time.

I want everything back to normal. In Burnz, go to Settings › Pause Burnz. Or switch Burnz off in iPhone Settings › Screen Time. Deleting Burnz also removes its restrictions.

My calories look wrong. Burnz reads Active Energy from Apple Health. Check iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Burnz and make sure Active Energy is on.

Something else? Contact us and we'll help.
